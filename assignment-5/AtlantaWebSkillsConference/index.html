Atlanta Web Skills Conference

Atlanta Web Skills Conference is a one-day learning event designed for people who are beginning their journey into web development. Attendees can take part in practical sessions focused on HTML, semantic markup, accessibility, and organizing content for real-world web projects.

The conference brings learners together in a supportive environment where they can build skills, ask questions, and learn from others who are also developing their knowledge of the web. Whether you are creating your first page or strengthening your understanding of semantic HTML, the event is designed to help you learn by doing.
